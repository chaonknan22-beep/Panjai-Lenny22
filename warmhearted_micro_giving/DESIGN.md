## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a joyful, affectionate micro-tipping experience. It replaces the transactional feel of payments with the tactile warmth of gifting a friend a sweet treat. The design aesthetic fuses playful, tactile shapes with clean, functional clarity—delivering an approachable interface that feels cozy, reassuring, and full of delight.

### Emotional Signature
- **Affectionate & Welcoming:** Generous whitespace, squishy pill containers, and warm strawberry-milk hues make giving feel intuitive, rewarding, and lighthearted.
- **Trustworthy & Transparent:** Clear numerical values, distinct button states, and uncluttered layouts reassure users during tipping and payout flows.
- **Playfully Tactile:** Micro-interactions mimic plush physical tokens, badges, and sweet treats that react to touch.

## Colors

The color system is rooted in soft strawberry milk, creamy vanilla custard, and sunny baked peach. Every surface maintains high legibility while steering clear of cold, sterile grays.

- **Primary (`#FB7185` - Rose Sorbet):** Primary call-to-actions, active tip amounts, heart counters, and focal points.
- **Secondary (`#FDBA74` - Warm Peach):** Celebratory badges, creator milestone bars, and secondary highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#F472B6` - Strawberry Blossom):** Decorative stickers, micro-animations, and community perks.
- **Neutral Base (`#FFFBF7` - Vanilla Cream):** Canvas foundation providing a soft, warm environment that reduces eye strain.
- **Neutral Surface (`#FFFFFF` - Pure Milk):** Foreground card containers, modular sheets, and floating drawers.
- **Neutral Ink (`#4C2D35` - Deep Cocoa Rose):** High-contrast, friendly dark tone used for primary typography and icons instead of harsh black.
- **Muted Ink (`#8C6E76` - Whipped Chestnut):** Subtitles, meta counters, timestamps, and placeholder copy.
- **Border & Dividers (`#FFE4E8` - Frosted Berry):** Low-contrast boundaries that organize content without breaking visual softness.

## Typography

The pairing between Bricolage Grotesque and Plus Jakarta Sans balances eccentric cheer with functional legibility.

- **Bricolage Grotesque:** Provides bouncy, handcrafted display headlines. Tight letter tracking and expressive ink traps turn donation totals, creator handles, and greeting headers into celebratory statements.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Clear, geometric, and softly rounded body text designed for effortless reading in bios, transactional receipts, and donor comment streams.
- **Localization Harmony:** Font sizes feature generous line heights (`1.45` to `1.55`) to comfortably host multi-script text without vertical clipping or awkward crowding.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts use a content-centric container system built on an 8px base rhythm. Giving flows and creator tipping pages remain focused within a central column, preventing interactive elements from scattering across wide displays.

### Structure
- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column layout with fixed bottom action sheets for tipping triggers, `margin-mobile` of 1rem, and tight 0.75rem element gutters.
- **Tablet (641px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with 1.25rem gutters, allowing side-by-side tipping options and recent donor feeds.
- **Desktop (> 1025px):** Max container width capped at 1120px with centered creator showcase cards, 12 columns, and 2rem outer margins.

Generous vertical padding (`space-xl`) isolates celebratory milestones from repetitive feed cards to keep the interface breezy and approachable.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through pillowy, ambient rose-tinted shadows paired with crisp milk-white foreground containers, avoiding cold gray drop-shadows.

### Shadow Styles
- **Resting Layer (Cards & Badges):** `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(251, 113, 133, 0.08)` produces a gentle floating appearance over the cream canvas.
- **Interactive Hover Layer (Tip Presets & Action Buttons):** `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(251, 113, 133, 0.18)` offers a plush, responsive lift effect.
- **Modals & Bottom Drawers:** `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(76, 45, 53, 0.12)` provides soft contrast for financial confirmation modals without dimming background joy.
- **Tactile Depress (Active Press):** `0 2px 4px 0 rgba(251, 113, 133, 0.12)` mimics physical push buttons.

## Shapes

The design uses rounded, pill-shaped elements (Level 3), turning standard containers into friendly, touchable pods.

- **Buttons, Badges, & Input Pills:** Set to full rounded pill values (`9999px`) or `rounded-xl` (3rem) for an inviting tactile profile.
- **Cards & Profile Modules:** Use `rounded-lg` (2rem) to soften content grouping.
- **Stickers & Tier Badges:** Use asymmetrical organic pill radiuses or smooth circular pods (`rounded-full`) to maintain a lively, sticker-book feel.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Tip Button:** Solid `#FB7185` background with pure white typography, full pill curvature (`rounded-full`), padded with `space-sm` vertically and `space-lg` horizontally. Hover yields an ambient pink bloom and slight upward translate (`-1px`).
- **Quick-Tip Preset Pills:** White milk surfaces with 1.5px `#FFE4E8` borders, displaying values like "30 ฿", "50 ฿", or "100 ฿". Selected state switches the border and background to `#FB7185` with white ink.

### Input Fields
- **Custom Amount & Donor Message:** Pillows of `#FFFFFF` wrapped with subtle `1.5px` border in `#FFE4E8`. Focus transition replaces the border with `#FB7185` backed by a 4px soft ring of `rgba(251, 113, 133, 0.15)`. Labels sit snugly inside or top-left in `label-md` `#8C6E76`.

### Cards & Tip Feeds
- **Creator Hero Card:** Pure white backdrop with `rounded-lg` (2rem) borders, carrying profile avatars with soft circular borders, milestone badges, and quick social links.
- **Donor Message Stream:** Light vanilla containers with soft rounded corners (`1.5rem`), featuring donor badges, warm peach highlight accents (`#FDBA74`), and message bubbles.

### Badges & Chips
- Compact pill-shaped tags (`label-sm`) using pastel tint fills like `#FFF1F2` and `#FFF7ED` with saturated borders and ink colors for member tiers, super-supporters, and thank-you notes.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Bulbous, friendly circular selectors featuring a soft bounce transition on check, colored in primary Rose Sorbet with clean white heart or check glyphs.
